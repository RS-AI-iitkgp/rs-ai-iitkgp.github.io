Photos for event "teachers-day-2026"
=====================================

1. Put the photos in this folder, named in display order:
   01.jpg, 02.jpg, 03.jpg, ...
2. List them in data/events.js under this event's "photos", e.g.
   photos: ["images/events/teachers-day-2026/01.jpg", "images/events/teachers-day-2026/02.jpg"]
   (or with captions: [{ src: "images/events/teachers-day-2026/01.jpg", caption: "..." }])

Recommended: JPG, longest side about 1600 px, under ~400 KB each.
Landscape photos look best in the gallery grid; every photo is shown
uncropped in the full-screen viewer.
